Studymatch Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Studymatch helps students find study partners and co-founders and find information about studying abroad. This page explains what information we collect and how we use it.

Information we collect

  • Account information: your email address and, if you sign in with Google, your name and email from your Google account. We do not receive your Google password.
  • Profile information you enter: name, age, major, school or university, target university, skills, goals and an optional profile photo.
  • Content you create: messages, files, photos, voice messages, essays, calendar entries and saved notes.

How we use it

  • To show your profile to other Studymatch members so they can find and contact you.
  • To deliver your messages, calls, invitations and notifications.
  • To keep the service safe, for example to remove abusive accounts.

We do not sell your personal information and we do not use it for advertising.

Who can see what

Your profile, files and essays are visible to other Studymatch members. Chats are visible only to the people in the conversation. Saved messages and your deadlines are visible only to you.

Where data is stored

Data is stored with our service provider Supabase. Sign-in with Google is provided by Google.

Your choices

You can edit your profile at any time and delete your own files and essays. To delete your account and data, contact us at the email below.
